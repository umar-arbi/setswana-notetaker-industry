Rep terms

Last updated: 26 November 2026

These terms cover the KwalaNotes Rep Programme. By joining, you agree to them, together with our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Questions: kwalanotes@gmail.com.

1. Joining

Anyone can join on our website. Join with your KwalaNotes account (email and password), or create one. Then you get your own rep code. You must be at least 18 and give us a true email address.

2. How a customer is linked to you

  • A new customer enters your rep code in the app before they pay.
  • The code only links the customer to you. It gives the customer no discount and no extra hours, and it does not change the PayPal price.
  • A link counts only if the customer starts their first PayPal payment, or PayPal records it, within 1 hour of entering the code. If the hour passes, the customer may enter the same code again and then pay.
  • A customer is linked to one rep for life. Once linked, a different rep's code is refused.
  • An account that has paid before cannot be linked, so a former paying customer does not count as new, even if they delete their account and sign up again.
  • You may use your own code when you subscribe yourself.

3. Commission

You earn a one-off commission for each linked customer, on that customer's first payment only:

  • Standard plan (US$9.99 a month, 20 hours): P20
  • Starter plan (US$4.99 a month, 8 hours): P10
  • Commission is fixed in Botswana Pula (BWP). It does not change with exchange rates or later payments.
  • Renewals and top-ups (extra hours) never earn commission.

4. When commission is payable

  • We pay every Saturday. Commission for payments made before that Saturday at 00:00 Botswana time becomes ready and is paid that Saturday.
  • If PayPal reverses or refunds the customer's payment, or the customer opens a dispute or chargeback, before we have paid the commission, the commission for that customer is cancelled.
  • Once a commission has been paid to you, we do not automatically take it back. We may flag it and may ask you to repay it if there was fraud.

5. How you are paid

We pay by Pay2Cell or e-Wallet to your mobile number, which you enter on your rep page, by the method you choose. You must keep your payout details correct. We are not responsible for a payment sent to the details you gave us. KwalaNotes pays its own sending fee. Any charges your mobile money provider makes are yours.

6. Your status

You are an independent person. You are not our employee, agent or partner, and you cannot make promises or sign anything for KwalaNotes. You are responsible for your own taxes on what you earn.

7. Rules

You must not:

  • spam or send unwanted messages, or post your code where it is not welcome;
  • make false or misleading claims about KwalaNotes, including promises about accuracy of the notes (notes are written by AI and may contain errors);
  • offer discounts, free hours or anything else on our behalf;
  • create fake accounts or accounts you control only to earn commission;
  • break the law or the rules of any platform you use.

8. Our rights

We may refuse, suspend or close a rep account, and cancel unpaid commission, if we believe there is fraud, spam, misleading claims or other abuse.

9. Changes and ending

We may change or end the programme by giving notice on our website or by email, at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Commission you have already earned stays payable under section 4. You may leave at any time by emailing us.

10. Your data

We store your email, rep code, linked customers, commission records and payout details (name and mobile number). We keep commission and payout records for 5 years after your last payment, for tax. See our Privacy Policy for details. You will see your linked customers only as a count. Deleting your KwalaNotes account erases your payout details; commission already earned stays payable if you sign in again with the same email. You will never see any customer's meetings or notes.

11. General

Botswana law applies, and the courts of Botswana have jurisdiction, as in our Terms of Service. Our liability to you under the programme is limited to the commission owed to you and not yet paid.
