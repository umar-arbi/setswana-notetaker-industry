Earn with KwalaNotes

Earn commission when your customers subscribe to KwalaNotes.

  1. Join with your KwalaNotes account. Use your email and password, or create an account.
  2. Your customer enters your code in the app and gets 1 free meeting hour. You earn when they subscribe and pay within 7 days after their free hour ends.
  3. You earn P20 for each Standard and P10 for each Starter first payment, once per customer. We pay every Saturday by Pay2Cell or e-Wallet to your mobile number for payments made before Saturday 00:00 Botswana time.
