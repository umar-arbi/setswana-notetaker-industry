Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions testers ask most. If something is still unclear, email us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.

Which languages does it understand?

KwalaNotes is built for mixed Setswana and English. The notes are written in English. It is still in testing, so the notes can contain mistakes — mostly in names.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. Nothing is added to your call. You record on your own machine and get notes afterwards.

Which call apps does it work with?

The app records your computer's sound, so it works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and WhatsApp desktop.

Any tips for clearer audio?

Use a headset if you can. It gives clearer sound and less echo. Keep the headset microphone close to your mouth, but not touching it.

What language are the notes in?

The summary and decisions are written in English. KwalaNotes is built for mixed Setswana and English and is still in testing, so the notes can contain mistakes — mostly in names.

What if the notes say "Please check"?

When the AI is not sure, the notes say "Please check" rather than guessing. Check those points with the people at the meeting.

What happens to my audio?

Audio is deleted from the server right after transcription.

How are the notes written?

To write the summary and decisions, the meeting transcript is sent over HTTPS to our AI providers (large language model services), whose servers may be outside Botswana and South Africa. Only transcript text is sent — your audio is never sent to them. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete a meeting?

Click Delete to remove a meeting from your computer.

What if I get a new laptop?

Your meetings are saved on your computer, not on our server. Before you change computers, open Settings → Move to a new PC and choose Create backup file. Pick a password and keep it safe — without it the backup can't be opened, and we can't recover it. Copy the file to your new computer, install KwalaNotes, and choose Restore from backup file. The backup has your meetings and notes, not the audio. It stays with you; KwalaNotes doesn't upload it anywhere.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows 10 or 11 only for now. Get KwalaNotes from the Microsoft Store.

How do I install it?

Get KwalaNotes from the Microsoft Store and click Get (or Install). Then open it and sign up or sign in.

How do I sign up?

Download the app and open it. Choose Sign up and enter your name, email and password. We email you a 6-digit code — type it in to finish. After you confirm the code, choose a plan (Starter or Standard) to start recording. KwalaNotes is still in testing, so please tell us if anything goes wrong.

Can I correct the notes?

Yes. You can edit the summary, decisions and speaker names. You can also add your own notes and press Update summary to fold them in. The AI is still in testing, so corrections are welcome.

Can I organise meetings?

Yes. You can put meetings into folders and drag them to reorder them.

Can it fill in meeting titles and names from my calendar?

Yes, from Outlook or Google Calendar. In Settings, choose Connect Microsoft calendar or Connect Google calendar (one at a time). When you start a recording, KwalaNotes fills in the title and attendee names of the meeting happening then, and you can change them. Access is read-only, and Disconnect removes it. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I import a recording from my phone?

Yes. Use Import an audio file to add a recording from your phone or another device. Supported formats are MP3, M4A, WAV, WEBM, OGG and AAC, up to 200 MB per file.

How do updates work?

The Microsoft Store keeps KwalaNotes up to date automatically. You can also check for updates in the Microsoft Store app under Library.

How much does it cost?

Starter: US$4.99 a month = 8 meeting hours per paid month. Standard: US$9.99 a month = 20 meeting hours per paid month. KwalaNotes is free to download, but you need a plan to record: you subscribe inside the app, paid through PayPal. See the terms.

Do I lose my saved meetings if my plan ends?

No. Your saved meetings stay on your computer and you can still read them. Recording and new notes need an active plan.

I have a gift code or a rep code. Where do I enter it?

On the plan screen in the app, in the Have a code? box. A rep code must be entered before you pay, and you must pay within one hour of entering it.

How do I pay?

Choose Subscribe with PayPal in the app. Both plans are paid monthly in US dollars and renew every month until you cancel. PayPal handles your card and bank details. We never see or store them.

What happens when my hours run out?

Hours are counted from one payment to the next (the “paid month”). When your hours run out, new meetings are not transcribed until your next paid month starts or you buy extra hours. The recording waits in the app for up to 14 days so you can retry then.

Can I buy more hours?

US$4.99 buys 8 extra hours through PayPal. This is a one-time payment and does not renew. You can buy extra hours only if you have an active paid plan (including a cancelled plan that is still paid up) and fewer than 2 meeting hours left. Extra hours expire at the end of that paid month and do not carry over. There are no refunds for extra hours, including unused extra hours. If an extra-hours payment is reversed by a chargeback, or a PayPal dispute about it is decided in your favour, those hours are removed. Opening a dispute on its own changes nothing.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel any time in the app or in PayPal. You won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of your paid month.

Can I switch plans?

Cancel your current plan, then subscribe to the other plan after your paid month ends.

Refunds

There are no refunds for unused time, including part of a month. If a second subscription is started by mistake while you already have a plan, we cancel it and refund its payment automatically. If we charge you twice by mistake in any other way, we refund the extra charge.

What if a payment fails?

PayPal tries again. If it still can't take the payment, the app asks you to update your payment method in PayPal.

How do I get in touch?

Email us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.
