Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions testers ask most. If something is still unclear, email us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.

Which languages does it understand?

KwalaNotes is built for mixed Setswana and English. The notes are written in English. It is still in testing, so the transcript can contain mistakes — mostly in names.

Does a bot join my meeting?

No. Nothing is added to your call. You record on your own machine and get notes afterwards.

Which call apps does it work with?

The app records your computer's sound, so it works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet and WhatsApp desktop.

Any tips for clearer audio?

Use a headset if you can. It gives clearer sound and less echo. Keep the headset microphone close to your mouth, but not touching it.

What language are the notes in?

The summary, decisions and action items are written in English. KwalaNotes is built for mixed Setswana and English and is still in testing, so the transcript can contain mistakes — mostly in names.

What if the notes are unclear?

When the AI is not sure, the notes say "unclear" rather than guessing. You can always check the transcript for the exact words.

What happens to my audio?

Audio is deleted from the server right after transcription.

How are the notes written?

To write the summary, decisions and action items, the meeting transcript is sent over HTTPS to DeepSeek, an AI model provider whose servers are in the People's Republic of China. Only transcript text is sent — your audio is never sent to DeepSeek. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete a meeting?

Click Delete to remove a meeting from your computer.

Which operating systems are supported?

Windows only for now. Download the .exe file from https://github.com/umar-arbi/setswana-notetaker-industry/releases/latest and double-click it to run. Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning because the app is not code-signed yet — click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I install it?

Download the .exe file from https://github.com/umar-arbi/setswana-notetaker-industry/releases/latest and double-click it. Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning because the app is not code-signed yet — click More info, then Run anyway. Then enter the access key you were given.

How do I sign up?

Download the app and open it. Choose Sign up and enter your name, email and password. We email you a 6-digit code — type it in to finish. Your first month is free with the code EISH. KwalaNotes is still in testing, so please tell us if anything goes wrong.

Can I correct the notes?

Yes. You can edit the summary, decisions, action items and individual transcript lines. You can also add your own notes and press Update summary to fold them in. The AI is still in testing, so corrections are welcome.

Can I organise meetings?

Yes. You can put meetings into folders and drag them to reorder them.

Can I import a recording from my phone?

Yes. Use Import an audio file to add a recording from your phone or another device. Supported formats are MP3, M4A, WAV, WEBM, OGG and AAC, up to 200 MB per file.

How do updates work?

KwalaNotes updates itself. It downloads new versions in the background and installs them the next time you close and reopen the app. Your meetings and settings are kept.
