Last updated: 25 November 2026

These terms govern your use of KwalaNotes, a desktop app that records meetings you choose to record, transcribes them, and writes notes. It is provided by KwalaNotes. You can reach us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.

By using the app, you agree to these terms.

Plans and payment

  • Starter: US$4.99 a month = 8 meeting hours per paid month.
  • Standard: US$9.99 a month = 20 meeting hours per paid month.
  • Paid month: Hours are counted from one payment to the next (the “paid month”).
  • When hours run out: When your hours run out, new meetings are not transcribed until your next paid month starts or you buy extra hours. The recording waits in the app for up to 14 days so you can retry then.
  • Auto-renewal: Both plans are paid monthly in US dollars through PayPal. Your subscription renews every month until you cancel.
  • Cancelling: You can cancel any time in the app or in PayPal. You won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of your paid month.
  • Switching plans: Cancel your current plan, then subscribe to the other plan after your paid month ends.
  • Gift codes: A gift code gives a fixed number of meeting hours until they are used up or the code expires.
  • Payment processor: PayPal handles your card and bank details. We never see or store them.

Extra hours

US$4.99 buys 8 extra hours through PayPal. This is a one-time payment and does not renew. You can buy extra hours only if you have an active paid plan (including a cancelled plan that is still paid up) and fewer than 2 meeting hours left. Extra hours expire at the end of that paid month and do not carry over. There are no refunds for extra hours, including unused extra hours. If an extra-hours payment is reversed by a chargeback, or a PayPal dispute about it is decided in your favour, those hours are removed. Opening a dispute on its own changes nothing.

No refunds

There are no refunds for unused time, including part of a month. If a second subscription is started by mistake while you already have a plan, we cancel it and refund its payment automatically. If we charge you twice by mistake in any other way, we refund the extra charge.

Disputes and chargebacks

If a subscription payment is reversed by a chargeback, or a PayPal dispute about it is decided in your favour, access ends straight away and the subscription is cancelled. Opening a dispute on its own changes nothing.

Do not use the app to record people without the consent required by law.
