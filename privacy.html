Privacy Policy — KwalaNotes

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains what KwalaNotes does with your information, in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.

Who we are

KwalaNotes is a Windows desktop app that records meetings you choose to record, transcribes them (including mixed Setswana and English), and writes notes. You can reach us at kwalanotes@gmail.com.

Your account

When you create an account we store:

  • your name, your email address, the date you signed up and whether your email is confirmed;
  • your password, only as a scrambled one-way hash (scrypt). We never store or see the password itself;
  • your plan or free-trial period, and how many minutes of audio you have used this month (to apply usage limits);
  • sign-in and email-confirmation codes, also only as hashes.

We send sign-up and password emails through Resend, an email service. Account details are stored on our service on Modal (United States).

What the app records

  • Your microphone: your own voice and, in an in-person meeting, the voices of everyone in the room.
  • Your computer audio: in an online meeting, the voices of the other participants.

The app records only meetings you choose to record. It reminds you to get everyone's consent first; getting that consent is your responsibility (see our Terms of Service). For an in-person meeting, only the microphone recording is uploaded; the computer-audio recording is not sent and is deleted with the rest of the audio.

Where your audio goes

When a recording is finished and you choose to write notes, the audio is uploaded over HTTPS (encrypted) to our transcription service on Modal Labs servers in the United States.

  • Our own copies: our service keeps the audio only as a temporary file long enough to hand it to a processing job, then deletes it.
  • While it is transcribed: Modal holds the audio as the input of that job and discards it after the job completes.
  • On your computer: the app deletes the audio from your computer as soon as the transcript is ready. If transcription fails, the audio stays on your computer for up to 14 days so you can press Retry, and is then deleted automatically.

Telling speakers apart: to label who said what, our service briefly analyses short pieces of the audio to tell voices apart. These voice patterns are used only during processing and are not kept.

Where your transcripts and notes are stored

  • Briefly on our service: the finished transcript and notes are held in our service's job storage on Modal until the app collects them. If the app doesn't collect them, they are deleted automatically within about 85 minutes of the upload.
  • Job records kept by Modal: Modal may keep information about each job for up to 7 days. Our job is designed to return no transcript content as its output.
  • On your computer: after that, the only copy of your transcripts and notes is on your own computer, stored encrypted.

AI features

To write your notes, we send the transcript text (never audio) over HTTPS to our AI providers, which may process it outside Botswana and South Africa. This happens when:

  • the transcript is cleaned up (fixing obvious recognition errors while keeping Setswana as spoken);
  • the summary, decisions and speaker names are written;
  • you add your own notes and update the summary.

How long our AI providers keep API data, and how they use it, is set by the provider's terms. Sending transcript text to our AI providers is a cross-border transfer (see below).

Calendar connection (optional)

You can choose to connect one calendar at a time in Settings: Microsoft (Outlook or Microsoft 365) or Google Calendar. You sign in on Microsoft's or Google's own page; KwalaNotes never sees your password. Access is read-only: for Google, the permission is to see your calendar events only, and KwalaNotes cannot change, add or delete anything.

  • When you start a recording, the app looks at the calendar meetings happening around that time. It reads the title, start and end time and attendees (names, email addresses and whether they accepted) only to find that meeting and label your recording. The app fills in the title and the attendees' names, and you can change or remove them.
  • The meeting title and attendee names are then handled like titles and names you type yourself: they are sent with the recording to write the notes (for example, to label speakers). Email addresses and other calendar details stay on your computer, are not stored or sent to us, and names are never made from email addresses.
  • The sign-in key is stored encrypted on your computer only. Choose Disconnect in Settings to delete it; for Google, the app also asks Google to cancel its access. You can also remove KwalaNotes from your Microsoft or Google account's app permissions at any time.
  • We never sell calendar data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

KwalaNotes' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

App diagnostics

The app keeps a small diagnostic log on your computer (for example, "upload started", error types). It never contains meeting audio, transcripts, notes, passwords or keys. It is only sent to us if you choose to copy it to us yourself ("Copy support info").

Updates

The app checks GitHub for new versions and downloads updates from there. This sends GitHub your internet address, like visiting any website.

Website: contact form

Your name, email and message are emailed to us so we can reply. They are not stored on our server; we keep them in our mailbox only as long as needed.

Website: anonymous tester feedback

The feedback page does not ask for your name or email. Your answers are emailed to us without them. To prevent abuse, our server briefly uses your internet address to limit how often messages can be sent; it does not store it. Please don't include confidential meeting content.

Website: visitor counting

We count page views per day and page. No cookies, no stored IP addresses, no tracking across sites. We honour Do Not Track.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never train our own models on your recordings or transcripts without your separate written opt-in. Whether our AI providers may use API data for their own purposes is set by the provider's terms.

Deleting your data

  • Meetings: you can delete any meeting in the app; this removes its transcript and notes from your computer.
  • Your account: email kwalanotes@gmail.com and we will delete your account details from our service.

Your rights under Botswana's Data Protection Act, 2024

If you are in Botswana, you have the right to:

  • Access: know what personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • Correction: have inaccurate data corrected;
  • Deletion: have your data deleted;
  • Objection: object to how your data is processed.

Contact kwalanotes@gmail.com to use these rights.

Sending data across borders

Your personal data leaves Botswana in these ways:

  • Transcription and accounts: on Modal servers in the United States.
  • AI-written notes: transcript text (never audio) is sent to our AI providers, whose servers may be outside Botswana and South Africa.
  • Email: account emails through Resend; contact and feedback messages to our Gmail mailbox.

The Data Protection Act, 2024 requires safeguards for cross-border transfers. We rely on those safeguards and will describe them here once confirmed by our lawyer.

South African users

If you are in South Africa, the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) also applies. Your rights are similar to those above; contact kwalanotes@gmail.com to use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
