Where your meeting goes

Four stops, in plain words. Read the full privacy policy for details.

  1. 1

    Your laptop

    You record on your own computer. Microphone and system audio are kept as separate tracks so speakers can be labelled "Me" and "Them".

  2. 2

    Secure cloud servers

    The audio is uploaded securely to our cloud servers for transcription.

  3. 3

    AI providers

    The transcript text is sent securely to our AI providers to write the notes. They process it under their own terms.

  4. 4

    Back to you, audio deleted

    Audio is deleted from our servers as soon as your notes are ready, and from your laptop once the notes arrive. If a recording runs past your meeting hours, only the untranscribed rest stays on your PC, encrypted, for up to 30 days so you can add it later; it is deleted when it is transcribed, when you delete the meeting, after 30 days, or when you uninstall KwalaNotes. We never use your meetings to train AI.
