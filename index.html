How it works

  1. Record

    Capture your microphone and your computer's audio as two separate tracks, so speakers can be labelled "Me" and "Them".

  2. Transcribe

    Audio is transcribed in short chunks with timestamps, in Setswana and English as spoken.

  3. Notes

    Get a summary, decisions and action items. When something is unclear, the notes say "unclear" instead of guessing.

Why it's different

  • Built for mixed Setswana and English

    It is built for mixed Setswana and English and writes the notes in English. It is still in testing: the transcript can contain mistakes, mostly in names, so check names before you send notes on.

  • No bot joins your call

    No bot is added to your meeting. You record on your own computer.

Privacy summary

  • Audio is deleted from our servers as soon as your notes are ready, and from your laptop once the notes arrive.
  • Microphone and system audio are kept as separate tracks so speakers can be labelled "Me" and "Them".
  • The audio is uploaded securely for transcription to servers in the United States. The transcript text is sent to an AI provider (DeepSeek, China) to write the notes. Nothing is used for training.
